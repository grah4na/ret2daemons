Home · Work · Nodes

ret2daemons is a private sanctuary for those who regard the computer as an object of study, not an appliance. Programmers, security researchers, systems thinkers, and tinkerers of every stripe gather here to build, break, and understand how things work.

Inquiry here follows effort, and standing is earned through contribution, never assertion. We prize rigour over rhetoric and depth over display.

If you are drawn to the architecture beneath the abstraction, and willing to labour for your understanding, you are among your own.

Who this is for

  1. You can describe a problem with what you tried, what failed, and the error output
  2. You’re curious enough to go down rabbit holes on your own
  3. You share what you find, since that’s how a community grows

Who this is not for

  1. Those looking for a shortcut or a “learning to hack in 30 days” guide
  2. Those asking where to begin without first searching, reading, or trying on their own
  3. Those seeking ways to target systems they don’t own or lack explicit permission to test

In short: If you have to ask how to start, you’re not ready yet.

How to join

Joining isn’t automatic. To get in:
